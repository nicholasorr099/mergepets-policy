Merge Pets — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026

Merge Pets ("the app", "we", "us") is a merge/idle game for children and families, published by NRO Dev. We designed Merge Pets to work fully offline and to collect as little data as possible. This policy explains what data the app may handle, when, and why.

The short version

  • If you just play the game, we collect no personal information about you at all. All game progress is stored only on your device.
  • Optional features (cloud backup, ads, and in-app purchases) involve limited data, described below. Cloud backup is off by default and is placed behind a grown-ups-only gate.
  • We never sell your data, and we never show ads or collect data based on a personal profile.

Information we do not collect

The app has no user accounts for gameplay, no chat, no user-generated content shared with others, and no analytics or tracking. With cloud backup turned off, the app makes no network connections that identify you.

Optional cloud backup (off by default, grown-ups only)

A parent or guardian can optionally turn on Cloud Backup so game progress can move to a new device. This feature sits behind a neutral age gate in the "Grown-ups" area. If, and only if, it is turned on:

  • What is collected: the email address of the Google account used to sign in, and your game progress (the game's save data — coins, pets, levels, and similar in-game values).
  • Why: solely to save and restore your progress across devices.
  • Where: stored on our backend provider, Supabase, encrypted in transit (HTTPS). It is linked to your Google account and is not shared with anyone else or used for advertising.
  • Deletion: use "Delete cloud backup" inside the app (Grown-ups → Cloud Backup) to erase it at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all on-device data.

Advertising

The app may show optional, reward-based ads (for example, to double offline coins) using Google AdMob. Ads are never forced and never interrupt play. Because Merge Pets is directed to a general audience that includes children, all ad requests are configured as child-directed and non-personalized. This means ads are not based on a personal profile of the user. Google's advertising SDK may process device identifiers to deliver and frequency-cap ads and to prevent fraud. See Google's policy for how Google handles this data.

In-app purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases (for example, in-game gems or an ads-free option), handled by Google Play Billing and processed through RevenueCat. Payment is handled by Google; we do not receive or store your payment card details. RevenueCat processes purchase history to validate purchases and restore them. Purchases are made in the grown-ups area behind the age gate.

Children's privacy

Merge Pets is intended to be safe for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the limited, optional cloud-backup data described above, which is reachable only by a parent or guardian passing the neutral age gate. Ads are child-directed and non-personalized. If you believe a child has provided personal information and you would like it removed, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Play entirely offline — no data leaves the device.
  • Leave cloud backup off (the default), or delete a backup in-app at any time.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions and purchases in Google Play.

Data retention

On-device game data stays until you clear it or uninstall the app. Cloud-backup data is kept until you delete it in-app or delete the associated Google account link; deleting the backup removes it from our servers.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
